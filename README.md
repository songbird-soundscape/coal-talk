# Coal Talk

*If you don't talk to your kids about coal… who will?*

A retro public-service-announcement about coal and its history, in three strands:

- **Economy & people:** the miners, the smoke, the CO₂, and what to do with old mines.
- **Peat landscapes:** the swamp forests coal comes from, then and now.
- **Science in the mines:** coal balls, fossil forests in mine roofs, and why mines should be open to science.

**Watch it:** https://songbird-soundscape.github.io/coal-talk/

One self-contained HTML page with pixel scenes, a coal-rank ladder and a timeline. Sources are listed on the page.
